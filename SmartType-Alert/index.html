One tiny alert, endless confidence

Instant sound notification

Each language has its own easily recognizable sound:

French · 1 beep Arabic · 2 beeps English · 3 beeps

Plays when you change layout in the active window, and reminds you of the current language when you start typing or return after a pause. Opening the app or moving the mouse between windows stays silent.

Layout error detection

Optional local analysis compares words to possible interpretations across installed keyboard layouts. If a mismatch is suspected, a small suggestion window appears.

You decide: accept to copy to clipboard and paste manually, or ignore. The program never replaces text or switches layout automatically.

Customizable to your workflow

  • Custom WAV sounds & volume
  • Adjustable pause before reminder
  • Enable/disable writing analysis
  • Show or hide correction suggestions
  • Exclude specific apps from analysis
  • Launch automatically at Windows login
  • Pause/resume from UI or tray icon

Clean interface, 3 languages

Enjoy a dark, organized interface with dedicated cards per language, clear toggle switches, and current keyboard layout display. Available in Arabic, French, and English – choose your preferred language.

Privacy comes first

SmartType Alert works completely offline. Writing is processed locally, never sent to external servers, and no text content is stored. It ignores password fields it can recognize, and lets you exclude sensitive applications entirely.

Who is it for?

Students, office workers, translators, writers – anyone who switches daily between Arabic, French, and English and wants a clear reminder of the current writing language without constant interruptions.

Your privacy stays on your device

No internet connection required. All analysis is done locally. SmartType Alert never saves your keystrokes and automatically ignores password fields it can identify. You can also exclude any sensitive application with one click.
