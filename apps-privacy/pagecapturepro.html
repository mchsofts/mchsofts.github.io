Privacy Policy – Page Capture Pro

Effective date: [Insert Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how Page Capture Pro (“the extension”) handles information when you use the extension. Page Capture Pro is designed to capture webpage screenshots and keep your data local to your Chrome profile.

Summary: Page Capture Pro does not upload your screenshots, URLs, or settings to any server. The developer does not receive this data. The extension does not sell or share user data with third parties and does not use it for advertising, tracking, or credit decisions.

1. Information Processed by the Extension

Page Capture Pro processes webpage content when you request a screenshot. It stores the resulting PNG, a thumbnail, the source tab’s URL when available, and capture metadata such as the filename, capture time, and image dimensions in your local Chrome profile.

The source URL is used for capture filenames and to identify the original page when you choose Capture Again.

2. Local Storage and History

The extension keeps up to 30 recent captures in local history. You can delete individual captures or clear the history from Settings.

Downloaded PNG files are saved separately and can be deleted from your device. The extension saves your settings locally.

3. Clipboard

When you choose Copy Image, the extension writes a screenshot to your clipboard. This action occurs locally on your device.

4. No Server Uploads

Page Capture Pro does not upload screenshots, URLs, or settings to any server. The developer does not receive this data.

5. No Sale, Sharing, or Advertising Use

The extension does not sell or share user data with third parties and does not use user data for advertising, tracking, or credit decisions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Captures and settings are retained locally in your Chrome profile until you delete them. You can delete individual captures or clear the capture history from Settings. Downloaded PNG files can be deleted from your device using your operating system’s file management tools.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Page Capture Pro, contact: contact@mchsofts.com
