01 Introduction

MCH Softs (“we”, “us”, or “our”) developed and operates NAYRO ADVENTURE.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

02 Information Stored Locally

NAYRO ADVENTURE stores certain game settings and progress locally on your device, including:

  • Level progress and unlocked levels
  • Scores, coins, gems, and saved checkpoints
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Update and advertising timing preferences

This information is stored on your device and is not directly sent to us.

03 Advertising

NAYRO ADVENTURE uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect or process certain information, such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • Advertising and interaction data

This information may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. Google may use cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies.

Advertising frequency: We use interstitial advertisements only at selected game transitions and apply a minimum interval between advertisements to avoid excessive interruptions.

For more information, please review:

How Google uses information from apps Google Privacy Policy

04 Children’s Privacy

NAYRO ADVENTURE is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Where required by applicable law, advertising and data collection settings should be configured in Google AdMob for child-directed treatment and applicable regional requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information may contact us so that we can review and address the request.

05 App Updates

The application may retrieve update information from our remote configuration file. This may include:

  • The latest application version
  • An update download URL
  • Update availability settings

The application does not use this information to identify users personally.

06 Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of these third-party providers.

07 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored application data. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
