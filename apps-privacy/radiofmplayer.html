01

Using the App

You can use the app without creating an account or signing in. The app searches the station list on your device and does not send your search terms to the developer or to the stations.

The app contains no ads and no tools for analyzing your usage or tracking your activity for marketing purposes.

02

Updating the Station List

The app connects to the mchsofts.com server over HTTPS when it opens, then every 15 minutes while it remains open, and whenever you request a manual update. This connection is used to download the station list, their stream URLs, and their logos. The app keeps a local copy of the list to display if the connection fails.

  • The list server receives the IP address visible to it when connecting, in order to send the response back to your device.
  • IP addresses are not stored in the update link's logs.
  • The app does not send your favorites, listening history, or recording files to this server.
03

Listening to Stations & Loading Logos

When you play a station or record its stream, the app connects to the streaming server operated by the station or its streaming provider. When displaying an externally hosted logo, the app connects to the image provider. These parties receive the IP address visible to them and the request data needed to send the stream or the image. The streaming provider may know which stream your device requested and when.

How these parties handle connection data and how long they retain it is governed by their own privacy policies. If you want to know what a particular streaming provider retains, or to request deletion of data it holds, contact them directly.

Some stations or logo servers use HTTP, which is an unencrypted connection. The app may temporarily accept, for 24 hours, certain stream certificates that cannot be verified so that you can play the station. The station list update connection remains subject to the usual certificate verification.

04

What Is Stored on Your Device

Everything listed below stays on your device only and is never uploaded to any server.

Data Why it is stored and how long it is retained
Favorite stations and volume level Stored so you find your choices when you reopen the app. Favorites remain until you remove them or clear app data, and the saved volume level is replaced whenever you change it.
Recently played stations The app keeps a list of up to 50 stations in Recent Plays. The oldest stations are replaced when the limit is reached, and you can clear the list by removing local app data.
Station list copy Stored so the directory can be shown when updating fails, and replaced when a new update succeeds.
Audio recordings When you press the record button, the app saves the stream audio to an MP3 file named with the station name and the recording date and time. The file remains on your device until you delete it.

The app records broadcast audio only and does not use your device's microphone. It does not upload recordings to the internet and does not sync favorites or listening history with an account or a server.

05

Accessing and Deleting Your Data

  • You can view and remove favorites from the Favorites page, and view your listening history from the Recent Plays page.
  • You can play recordings or open their folder from the Recordings page. The Delete button moves a recording to the Windows Recycle Bin. To delete it from there, delete it from the Recycle Bin as well.
  • To clear favorites, listening history, settings, and the saved list all at once, you can remove local app data from Windows. Save a copy of any recordings you want to keep before removing app data.
