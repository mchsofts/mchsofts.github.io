Privacy Policy

At MCH SOFTS, we build Windows software, Android applications, and Chrome extensions with privacy as a core principle. This page explains our general approach to data across our website and projects. Because our apps and programs are designed for all ages — including children — and vary in nature, each individual product provides its own privacy policy that you should read before installing or using it.

Scope of this policy

  • This general policy applies to the MCH SOFTS website and to our projects as a whole.
  • It is not a substitute for the specific privacy policy of each app or program.
  • Every product page links to its own privacy policy, which describes exactly what that product does with data.
  • If a product-specific policy conflicts with this page, the product-specific policy governs for that product.

General principles

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.
  • Most of our software works fully offline, with no telemetry or tracking.
  • We only collect the minimum information necessary to deliver and improve our products.
  • We never store your keystrokes, document content, or private files.

Windows software

Our Windows programs are designed to run locally. Unless explicitly stated in a product's own privacy policy, they do not transmit data to external servers. Any optional diagnostic or usage data is anonymized, opt-in, and used solely to improve stability and performance.

Android applications

Android apps published by MCH SOFTS may be free, ad-supported, paid, or open-source, and may be intended for all ages. Depending on the app, the following may apply — but always check the app's own privacy policy:

  • Ad-supported apps may use Google's advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can reset or limit this ID in your device settings.
  • Free apps may collect anonymous crash reports and basic analytics to help us fix issues.
  • Paid apps typically do not display ads and may collect no personal data at all.
  • Apps distributed through Google Play are subject to Google Play's own policies in addition to ours.
  • Apps directed at children comply with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and GDPR-K) as described in each app's specific policy.

Chrome extensions

Our Chrome extensions operate with the minimum permissions required for their functionality. We do not collect browsing history, keystrokes, or any personal information unless a specific extension explicitly requests and explains such data collection. In those cases, data processing is described on the extension's store page and in its own privacy policy.

Data we may collect

  • Contact information you voluntarily provide (e.g., when emailing us at contact@mchsofts.com).
  • Anonymous usage and crash analytics for certain apps (only if enabled and clearly disclosed in that product's policy).
  • Purchase records for paid products, managed by the platform (Google Play, etc.) — we do not store payment details.

Children's privacy

Some of our products are designed for all ages, including children. We take children's privacy seriously. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is strictly necessary for the product to function, and we never sell children's data. Each child-directed app provides a specific privacy policy describing its practices and any parental controls. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights & contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. For any privacy-related question, contact us at contact@mchsofts.com. We respond within 30 days.
